Peach Shortcake with Basil Whipped Cream

Description:

Simple-to-make drop biscuits that serve as the shortcakes are buttery, flaky, subtly sweet, and pretty darn delicious all on their own. But when you split them and stuff them with juicy peaches, a giant dollop of whipped cream, and fresh basil, they get even dreamier. Put this at the top of your list of peach desserts to make while they’re in season. You’ll be glad you did!

Ingredients:

  • FOR THE BISCUITS:
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 Tbsp. baking powder
  • 3 Tbsp. sugar
  • 1/2 tsp. kosher salt
  • 1 1/2 sticks (12 Tbsp.) cold salted butter, cut into pieces
  • 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
  • FOR THE PEACHES:
  • 4 peaches (about 1 1/2 lb.)
  • 2 Tbsp. sugar
  • Grated zest and juice of 1 small orange
  • FOR THE CREAM:
  • 1 (7-ounce) container plain Greek yogurt (about 3/4 cup)
  • 1 cup cold heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • Chopped fresh basil, for sprinkling
  • 1/2 tsp. vanilla extract (optional)

Directions:

  1. For the biscuits: Preheat the oven to 400˚F. Combine the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a food processor. Add the butter and pulse until the mixture resembles tiny pebbles. (You can also do this in a bowl with a pastry cutter.) Gradually pulse in the buttermilk until the dough just comes together and is no longer crumbly.
  2. Drop mounds of dough (about 1/2 cup each) onto 2 unlined baking sheets. Bake, rotating the baking sheets halfway through, until the biscuits are golden brown, about 15 minutes. Let cool completely on the baking sheets.
  3. Meanwhile, for the peaches: Slice the peaches into thin wedges and place in a large bowl. Sprinkle with the sugar, then add the orange zest and juice and stir gently. Set aside until juicy, 15 to 20 minutes.
  4. For the cream: Combine the yogurt, heavy cream, sugar, and vanilla extract (if using) in a large bowl and beat with a mixer on high speed until stiff (it won’t get as stiff as regular whipped cream).
  5. Split the biscuits and fill with the peaches and cream. Sprinkle with basil.
  6. Enjoy!
